Meet Our Founder, Jun Miao

AI (愛) Future You was founded by Jun Miao, whose career has been built at the frontier of AI-powered products—from computational photography and AI-driven imaging systems to on-device machine learning for AR/VR/XR devices at industry leaders like Meta and Apple. After over 15 years of turning cutting-edge AI research into foundational products used by millions, Jun set out to build something profoundly more personal.

Having spent his career engineering how machines perceive the physical world, his ambition shifted toward how machines can understand human connection. He realized the next great technological leap isn't just about processing data faster—it's about creating technology that understands you.

AI (愛) Future You is Jun's vision for what comes next. It is the culmination of his hardware and machine learning expertise applied to creating empathetic digital twins. These intelligent agents are designed to grow and adapt alongside you, seamlessly representing your voice in daily communications, and ultimately evolving into a comforting, highly capable guide from your future.
