Open Positions

Software Architect

Engineering & AI Systems

Full-time / Equity Focus

We need a visionary architect to lay the foundation for our agentic communication networks. You will design systems capable of secure, empathetic, and continuous learning, transforming LLMs into personalized, temporal proxies.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Design highly scalable backend architectures for autonomous AI agents.
  • Implement secure data-streaming and context-retention mechanisms.
  • Collaborate on ML pipelines for personalized "Future You" predictive models.
  • Establish core engineering protocols for our seed-stage MVP.

Requirements:

  • 5+ years of software architecture/engineering experience.
  • Deep knowledge of LLMs, agentic frameworks (e.g., LangChain, AutoGen), and cloud infrastructure.
  • Ability to thrive in an unstructured, fast-paced startup environment.
  • Passion for ethical AI and data privacy.

Social Media Campaigner

Marketing & Community

Full-time / Equity Focus

Art Designer

UI/UX & Brand Identity

Full-time / Equity Focus

As our foundational designer, you will define how our users visualize and interact with their "Future Self". You will ensure our brand avoids the cold, robotic clichés of AI, focusing instead on warmth, golden-hour aesthetics, and human empathy.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Own the end-to-end UI/UX design for our web platform and future mobile apps.
  • Develop our visual brand identity, illustrations, and digital assets.
  • Design intuitive interfaces for complex human-to-AI interactions.
  • Collaborate closely with engineering to ensure seamless implementation.

Requirements:
